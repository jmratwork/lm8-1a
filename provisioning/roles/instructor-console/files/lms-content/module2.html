Module 2 — Analysing Content for Red Flags

Even when the sender appears legitimate, the email body often contains detectable indicators of a phishing attempt.

Urgency and Fear Tactics

Phishing emails exploit psychological pressure. Look for language such as:

  • "Your account will be suspended within 24 hours"
  • "Immediate action required — failure to respond will result in…"
  • "URGENT: Verify your identity now"

Legitimate IT systems rarely threaten immediate suspension via email without prior warning.

Grammatical and Stylistic Errors

While AI-generated phishing has improved, many attacks still contain:

  • Spelling mistakes and grammatical errors
  • Inconsistent capitalisation or punctuation
  • Awkward or overly formal phrasing
  • Generic salutations ("Dear User", "Dear Customer")

Suspicious URLs

Before clicking any link, hover over it to preview the actual URL. Red flags:

  • Domain does not match the organisation
  • IP address instead of domain name (http://192.168.x.x/login)
  • HTTP instead of HTTPS for login pages
  • Excessive redirects or URL shorteners (bit.ly, t.co)

Mismatched Branding

Logos, colours, and layout that do not match the real organisation’s branding, or images that fail to load (broken image placeholders).
